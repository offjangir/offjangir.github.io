FAQ

What is this?

This is a space for exploring ideas about intelligence, perception, and learning both in humans and machines. I write about research, theory, and the connections between how minds work.

Why human + machine?

Understanding intelligence requires looking at both biological and artificial systems. Each offers insights into the other, and the parallels are often more revealing than the differences.

How often will you post?

I'll post when I have something meaningful to share—no fixed schedule. Quality over frequency.

Can I cite/share?

Yes, please cite appropriately if you reference these posts. Sharing is welcome—feel free to link or discuss.
